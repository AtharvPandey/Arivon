# Arivon Product Development Bible

# Volume 1 — Chapter 1
## Product Vision & Philosophy

> This is Chapter 1 of the Arivon Product Development Bible.

## 1. Vision

Arivon is an enterprise-grade, AI-ready, multi-tenant School ERP platform built to manage the complete lifecycle of a school from admission to alumni.

The platform is designed so every stakeholder works within a role-specific workspace while all activities are governed by a centralized permission engine, audit system, workflow engine, and notification engine.

## 2. Goals

- One platform for thousands of schools.
- One codebase with complete tenant isolation.
- Configurable workflows instead of hard-coded logic.
- Enterprise-grade security.
- AI-ready architecture.
- Modular domain-driven design.

## 3. Core Principles

1. Every action is permission protected.
2. Every record belongs to exactly one school.
3. Operational work is performed only by operational roles.
4. Administrators supervise rather than execute departmental tasks.
5. Every important action creates an audit trail.
6. Every module integrates with notifications and reporting.

## 5. Product Pillars

### Security
RBAC, MFA readiness, audit logs, tenant isolation.

### Scalability
Support thousands of schools using the same platform.

### Flexibility
Custom roles, custom departments, configurable permissions.

### Usability
Role-based dashboards with only relevant actions visible.

### Intelligence
Architecture prepared for AI assistants, analytics, predictions, and automation.

## 6. Success Criteria

- Fast onboarding of schools.
- Minimal training required for staff.
- Centralized visibility for administrators.
- Department-specific productivity.
- Reliable reporting and auditability.
